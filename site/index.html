Governed work, composed

Work that survives the session.

Vuoro packages shared work, execution, knowledge, and audit capabilities behind one versioned service while keeping authority in the tools that own it—and machine effects on the machine that performs them.

Composition layer in active development

The protocol, client/service packaging boundary, bootstrap runtime, and neutral deployment assets work today. Released production adapters and environment composition are the next boundary.

One system, three paths.

Local, remote, and served are communication modes, not rival sources of truth. They can be enabled side by side, and a served request can use a remote authority while its worker keeps Git and filesystem effects local.

Local

Direct and repo-local

An agent calls the owning CLI. SQLite state, claim recovery, worktrees, and filesystem effects remain on that machine.

Remote

Shared domain authority

The same owner contract reaches PostgreSQL so agents and operators on different hosts coordinate against shared state.

Served

One versioned edge

The transport-only client calls Vuoro service for identity, compatibility, catalog discovery, and generic invocation through pinned adapters.

The communication path.

The service composes capabilities; it does not absorb their state machines. The cockpit is a window and dispatch surface, not a database with a privileged shortcut.

Failure is part of the proof.

The meaningful end-to-end scenario is not a happy-path demo. It shows that invalid work cannot settle state, and that recovery preserves evidence without accepting stale ownership.

Create and claim

sprintctl records the work item. A claim ID and secret token—not a label, branch, or hostname—prove the active owner.

Dispatch in bounds

actionq records the action. Dispatch creates a bounded worktree, invokes one worker, and applies policy and verification gates.

Reject invalid work

A failed worker or invalid result is recorded, but is not published and cannot close the work item.

Resume or recover

The owner resumes with private recovery proof, or an explicit handoff rotates ownership so stale proof can no longer settle.

Verify and settle

Only an independently verified result is published, completed through the owning CLI, and released from its claim.

Observe evidence

Audit evidence and cockpit projections show the work, claim, dispatch, rejection or success, and recovery lineage.
